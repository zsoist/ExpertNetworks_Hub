Executive Summary

What the landscape looks like now

Four structural dynamics shaping the expert network market in 2025–2026.

Market Structure Is Consolidating

The industry has grown to an estimated $2.5–5B depending on methodology, with 11,200+ companies using expert networks globally. A core tier of roughly 15 institutional-grade firms handles the majority of volume, while dozens of niche and regional players serve specific verticals or geographies.

Implication: Scale advantages are compounding — smaller firms face margin and access pressure.

AI Is Reshaping the Competitive Stack

Every major provider shipped AI features between 2024–2026. AlphaSense reached $500M ARR with agentic research tools. GLG, Guidepoint, and Dialectica each launched AI-assisted matching, synthesis, or workflow products. AI is moving from differentiator to table stakes.

Implication: Buyer expectations for AI-augmented delivery are accelerating faster than most providers can ship.

Transcript & Content Assets Are a Battleground

Third Bridge distributes its library through six AI partners. AlphaSense claims 240K+ transcripts across 500M+ documents. Content-rich models create recurring usage and switching costs that call-only providers struggle to match.

Implication: Firms without a content asset will increasingly compete on price and speed alone.

Buyer Sophistication Is Increasing

PE firms, consultancies, and corporate strategy teams now compare providers across compliance posture, AI tooling, vertical depth, and integration fit — not just cost per call. Procurement is becoming more structured and multi-vendor.

Implication: Transparent comparison and compliance credibility are now buying criteria, not afterthoughts.

Intelligence Stream

Recent Competitor Signals

Select public-source signals illustrating market movement. Each labeled as editorial synthesis.

Oct 2025 AI Launch

AlphaSense reaches $500M ARR, launches agentic Deep Research

AlphaSense disclosed $500M in annual recurring revenue and launched Deep Research, an agentic workflow product, alongside AI-driven Channel Checks that automate expert interviewing at scale.

Raises the ceiling for what buyers expect from a research platform — providers without agentic capabilities may face repositioning pressure.
Oct 2025 Product

Guidepoint launches Guidepoint360 and AskGP

Guidepoint released its unified platform Guidepoint360 with AskGP, an AI assistant for cross-transcript search and synthesis, plus AI-powered compliance moderation.

Signals that traditional concierge providers are investing heavily in platform parity — the concierge-vs-platform distinction is blurring.
2025 Distribution

Third Bridge opens content distribution to six AI partners

Third Bridge made its research library available through Claude, Aiera, Hebbia, Portrait Analytics, Finster, and Rogo — a notable shift toward open distribution of expert-derived content.

Content owners may gain leverage by becoming upstream data suppliers to multiple AI platforms — a new monetization and moat strategy.
Aug 2025 Milestone

Dialectica surpasses $100M revenue with 1,400 employees

Dialectica reported exceeding $100M in revenue, maintaining a 70% lifetime CAGR over five consecutive years on the FT 1000. The firm also appointed Jason Howard (ex-Tegus GC, 13 years SEC) as Chief Legal Officer.

Growth-stage firm maturing into institutional credibility — the compliance hire signals readiness for larger, more regulated enterprise accounts.
Sep 2024 Funding

AlphaSights raises $400M at $4.2B valuation

AlphaSights closed a $400M capital raise valuing the firm at $4.2B, underscoring investor confidence in the expert network model. The firm was also named an FT Growth Champion for 2026.

Validates the market thesis at scale — signals that expert networks are attracting growth-equity capital typically reserved for enterprise SaaS.
May 2023 Regulatory

Capvision HKEX IPO stalled following CCTV regulatory investigation

Capvision's planned Hong Kong IPO was paused after a CCTV broadcast investigated the firm's operations in mainland China, highlighting regulatory risks for cross-border expert network providers.

Geopolitical and regulatory exposure is a material competitive variable — compliance credibility matters more in cross-border contexts.

Methodology

Intelligence Sourcing & Evidence Framework

All intelligence is derived from public sources. No proprietary, confidential, or insider data is used.

Sources include: provider websites, product pages, press releases, newsroom updates, regulatory filings, market reports from Inex One, BRI, and Silverlight Research, and trusted third-party reporting. Change tracking is applied to public web properties where available.

Verified

Confirmed through multiple independent public sources or official filings. Example: funding rounds disclosed in SEC filings and press coverage.

Company-Stated

Based on the provider's own disclosures — press releases, product pages, or executive statements. Not independently verified.

Inferred Editorially

Synthesized from multiple data points but not directly confirmed. Requires analyst judgment. Labeled clearly as editorial assessment.

Weak Signal / Monitor

Early-stage indicators that may or may not materialize. Included for tracking purposes, not actionable without further confirmation.
