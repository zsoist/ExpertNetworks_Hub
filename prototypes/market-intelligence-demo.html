Executive Summary

What the landscape looks like now

Four structural dynamics shaping the expert network market in 2025–2026.

Market Consolidation

An estimated $2.5–5B market with 11,200+ corporate users. Roughly 15 institutional-grade firms handle most volume; dozens of niche players serve specific verticals.

Scale advantages are compounding — smaller firms face margin pressure.

AI Is Now Table Stakes

Every major provider shipped AI features in 2024–2026. AlphaSense hit $500M ARR with agentic tools. GLG, Guidepoint, and Dialectica each launched AI matching or synthesis products.

Buyer expectations for AI-augmented delivery are outpacing provider roadmaps.

Content & Transcript Arms Race

Third Bridge distributes its library through six AI partners. AlphaSense claims 240K+ transcripts. Content-rich models create switching costs that call-only providers cannot match.

Providers without a content asset will compete on price and speed alone.

Buyer Procurement Is Maturing

PE firms and corporate strategy teams now evaluate providers across compliance, AI tooling, vertical depth, and integration fit — not just cost per call.

Transparent comparison and compliance credibility are buying criteria, not afterthoughts.

Recent Competitor Signals

Oct 2025 AI Launch

AlphaSense reaches $500M ARR, launches agentic Deep Research

Disclosed $500M ARR. Launched Deep Research (agentic workflows) and AI-driven Channel Checks that automate expert interviewing.

Raises the ceiling for buyer expectations — providers without agentic capabilities face repositioning pressure.
Oct 2025 Product

Guidepoint launches Guidepoint360 and AskGP

Unified platform launch with AskGP (AI cross-transcript search and synthesis) and AI-powered compliance moderation.

Traditional concierge providers investing heavily in platform parity — the concierge-vs-platform line is blurring.
2025 Distribution

Third Bridge opens content distribution to six AI partners

Research library now available through Claude, Aiera, Hebbia, Portrait Analytics, Finster, and Rogo — a shift toward open content distribution.

Content owners can become upstream data suppliers to AI platforms — a new moat and monetization strategy.
Aug 2025 Milestone

Dialectica surpasses $100M revenue with 1,400 employees

Exceeded $100M revenue, 70% lifetime CAGR, five consecutive FT 1000 appearances. Appointed Jason Howard (ex-Tegus GC, 13 years SEC) as CLO.

Growth-stage firm maturing into institutional credibility — compliance hire signals readiness for regulated enterprise accounts.
Sep 2024 Funding

AlphaSights raises $400M at $4.2B valuation

Closed $400M raise at $4.2B valuation. Named FT Growth Champion 2026, reporting 70% CAGR.

Validates the market thesis at scale — expert networks attracting growth-equity capital typically reserved for enterprise SaaS.
May 2023 Regulatory

Capvision HKEX IPO stalled following CCTV regulatory investigation

Planned HKEX IPO paused after CCTV investigated mainland China operations — highlighting regulatory risk for cross-border expert network providers.

Geopolitical exposure is a material competitive variable — compliance credibility matters more in cross-border contexts.

Methodology

Intelligence Sourcing & Evidence Framework

All intelligence is derived from public sources. No proprietary, confidential, or insider data is used.

Sources: provider websites, product pages, press releases, regulatory filings, market reports (Inex One, BRI, Silverlight Research), and third-party reporting. Web-property change tracking applied where available.

Verified

Confirmed through multiple independent public sources or official filings.

Company-Stated

Provider's own disclosures — press releases, product pages, executive statements. Not independently verified.

Inferred Editorially

Synthesized from multiple data points, not directly confirmed. Requires analyst judgment.

Weak Signal / Monitor

Early indicators that may not materialize. Tracked for awareness, not actionable without confirmation.
