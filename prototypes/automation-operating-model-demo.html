What the System Produces

Two recurring analysis outputs

Each runs on a weekly cadence and produces structured, evidence-labeled intelligence.

Weekly

News Intelligence Workflow

Curates and verifies recent developments across expert network providers — product launches, funding, partnerships, regulatory events, leadership changes.

Key Inputs

  • Provider newsrooms and press releases
  • Regulatory filings and disclosures
  • Third-party market reporting
  • Product page and website changes

Output

Structured signal cards with date, provider, category, summary, evidence label, and strategic implication.

Weekly

Competitive Advantage Analysis

Analyzes how providers differentiate across key buyer-relevant dimensions — AI capability, content assets, compliance posture, service model, geography.

Key Inputs

  • Current provider profile data (33 firms)
  • Recent intelligence signals
  • Compare-dimension framework (45+ fields)
  • Public financial and scale disclosures

Output

Competitive matrices, advantage stacks, and strategic lens commentary — all labeled as editorial synthesis.

Saturday Run Cycle

Weekly execution cadence

A structured sequence runs each Saturday. Each step is logged and reviewable.

1. Source scan
Check provider newsrooms, press pages, filings, and tracked third-party sources for new material.
2. Signal extraction
Identify discrete events: launches, funding, hires, partnerships, compliance updates, market sizing.
3. Evidence labeling
Tag each signal with confidence level: verified, company-stated, inferred, or weak signal.
4. Deduplication & filtering
Remove duplicates, discard low-relevance noise, merge related signals where appropriate.
5. Competitive synthesis
Update advantage stacks and comparative assessments based on new signal material.
6. Output generation
Produce structured JSON content: news signals, provider updates, compare-dimension changes.
7. Human review
Analyst reviews all outputs for accuracy, editorial tone, and factual restraint before publication.

Agent Architecture

Layered processing model

Conceptual workflow layers — each handles a distinct responsibility in the intelligence pipeline.

1
Source Intake
Reads from provider websites, newsrooms, filings, and monitored third-party sources.
2
Filtering
Applies relevance criteria — industry scope, recency, materiality threshold.
3
Evidence Review
Assesses source quality and assigns confidence labels per the verification rubric.
4
Categorization
Tags signals by type: product, funding, regulatory, M&A, compliance, talent, market sizing.
5
Synthesis
Generates implications, competitive context, and connects signals to the advantage framework.
6
Output Formatting
Structures results as JSON content files matching the site's content schema.
7
Human Review
Final analyst pass — editorial judgment, factual verification, tone calibration.

This is a prototype workflow model. It does not describe a deployed autonomous agent system.

Verification Rubric

How confidence is assessed

Every signal is evaluated against five dimensions before inclusion.

Dimension High Confidence Medium Low / Monitor
Source Quality Official filing, SEC disclosure Company press release Single third-party mention
Recency Within 30 days Within 90 days Older than 6 months
Evidence Directness Named source, public record Attributed statement Inference from indirect data
Strategic Relevance Affects competitive positioning Relevant to market context Peripheral or speculative
Confidence Label Verified Company-Stated Inferred / Weak

Human-in-the-Loop

Where analyst judgment matters

Automation handles collection and structuring. Humans handle meaning.

Judgment calls that require a human

  • Whether a signal is materially significant or noise
  • How to frame competitive implications without overstating
  • Whether inferred signals meet the threshold for inclusion
  • Editorial tone — avoiding hype, speculation, or false precision
  • Final approval before any content is published

What automation handles well

  • Scanning multiple source pages for new material
  • Extracting structured fields from unstructured text
  • Applying consistent categorization labels
  • Formatting output to match content schema
  • Flagging potential duplicates and conflicts

No output is published without human review. The system proposes; the analyst decides.
